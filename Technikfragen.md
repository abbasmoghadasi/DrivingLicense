## Key Lighting Functions
- **Standlicht (Position Lights)**:
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/67c34b97-1411-4ef7-9026-34d4cdac38b2)

  - First position on the light switch.
  - Check functionality using the green control light or by stepping out to visually confirm. 

- **Abblendlicht (Low Beam)**:
  - Used for night driving and poor visibility conditions.
  - Always leave on during bad weather for safety. 

- **Nebelscheinwerfer (Fog Lights)**:
  - Green control light indicates operation.
  - Designed to illuminate the road without blinding other drivers, used in fog, rain, or snow. 

- **Nebelschlussleuchte (Rear Fog Light)**:
  - Bright red light, only to be used in visibility under 50 meters to avoid blinding other drivers. 

- **Fernlicht (High Beam)**:
  - Can be activated with the blinker lever; ensure low beam is also on to avoid confusion. 

- **Blinklicht (Turn Signal)**:
  - Used for signaling turns and emergencies; important for safety on the road. 

- **Rückfahrscheinwerfer (Reverse Light)**:
  - Activated by putting the car in reverse; typically white lights at the back. 

- **Bremslicht (Brake Light)**:
  - Must be checked for functionality; important for safety and communication with other drivers. 

## Tire Maintenance
- **Profiltiefe (Tread Depth)**:
  - Minimum required depth is **1.6 mm**. 

- **Luftdruck (Tire Pressure)**:
  - Check the manual or sticker inside the door for correct pressure levels. 

- **Beschädigung (Damage)**:
  - Visually inspect tires for any visible damage or foreign objects. 

## Fluid Levels
- **Scheibenwischanlage (Windshield Washer Fluid)**:
  - Ensure it is filled regularly to maintain visibility. 

- **Kühlflüssigkeit (Coolant)**:
  - Check for adequate levels and ensure frost protection is included. 

- **Ölstand (Oil Level)**:
  - Check using the dipstick; ensure the vehicle is level for an accurate reading. 

## Additional Tips
- Always refer to the **owner's manual** for specific vehicle details.
- If unsure about an answer, suggest checking the manual to demonstrate preparedness. 
- Stay calm and confident during the exam; first impressions are crucial. 

## Conclusion
These notes summarize the key technical aspects that may be questioned during the driving exam. Familiarity with these topics will enhance confidence and performance during the test. Good luck!
